# Factur-X Engine commercial evaluation discontinued

The Factur-X Engine Intake evaluation and commercial test ended on 24 August
2026. The checkout is closed, no new evaluation or commercial keys are issued,
and there is no active paid offer.

Previously issued evaluation keys retain only the expiry encoded in those keys.
They carry no service-level agreement, support response time, maintenance
promise, security remediation deadline, regulatory monitoring obligation, or
guarantee of fitness for production use.

Factur-X Engine is discontinued. Version `2.1.0` is the final frozen release and
will receive no support, security fixes, regulatory monitoring, or standards
updates.
